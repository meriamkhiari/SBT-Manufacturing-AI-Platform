Smart Brain Technologie

Smart Brain Technologie (SBT) is an industrial subcontracting company specializing in the manufacture of electrical harnesses and electrical cabinets. With years of expertise in electrical wiring, we are committed to meeting the most demanding industrial requirements through advanced technology and highly qualified teams.
